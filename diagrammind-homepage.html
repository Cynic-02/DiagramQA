The thesis

A DIAGRAMIS A GRAPH.

A question is an edge you remove and ask someone to put back. That is the whole idea — and it is why this works on a cell, an atom, a circuit, an orbit or a food web without changing a line of the model.

Structure first, wording second. Everything else follows from the graph.

The pipeline

SIX AGENTS,ONE RUN.

01Ingest
Reads the file, normalises it, finds the drawing.
02Vision
Parses entities, relationships and spatial layout into a graph.
03Generator
Writes questions conditioned on each Bloom level.
04Answerer
Answers each question independently, from the structure only.
05Verifier
A second agent checks every answer and flags what it cannot support.
06Reporter
Filters, edits, exports. You get a set, not a draft.
Coverage
