Login Page Background Redesign & Scroll Dynamics

Replacing the legacy 2D quad particle grid with the "Quantum Diagram Neural Mesh" — a living, scroll-driven 3D knowledge matrix that synthesizes raw entropy into structured diagram geometry as you scroll through the login journey.

SCROLL JOURNEY SIMULATION (BEAT 0 → BEAT 3) Beat 0.00 (0% Scroll)
💡 Why Redesign This Graph?

The old login background used simple flat square quads held in a static wordmark beat. DiagramMind is an AI diagram generator — the background should visually tell the story of raw thought transforming into clear diagram structures.

🌀 Scroll Progression Dynamics

Beat 0: Unstructured floating quantum data motes.
Beat 1: Snapping into 6 agent color clusters (Parser, Logic, Diagram, Question, Render).
Beat 2: Connecting into 3D diagram vector geometry.
Beat 3: Locking into a radiant focal emblem behind the login card.

⚡ Performance & Technical Rationale

Uses custom WebGL shaders with lerped uniform interpolation in vertex space (`uBeat`). Zero per-frame JavaScript array loops over nodes. Pauses render loop when tab or scroll position is off-screen.
