   192→  1. **Particle starfield** — new `<Starfield>` component renders a single `THREE.Points` (NOT individual meshes) of 520 points on a spherical shell (radius 25–40) with a `BufferGeometry` built once via `useMemo`. Per-vertex colors mix 65% cool neutral white (`#e2e8f0`) + 35% emerald tint (`#9ae6c8`), each brightness-jittered. `pointsMaterial` uses `size=0.035`, `sizeAttenuation`, `transparent`, `opacity=0.5`, `depthWrite=false`, `vertexColors`, `toneMapped=false`.    193→  2. **Data-flow pulses** — added 23 small emissive spheres (one per edge) as children of the rotating `<group>` so they stay aligned with the edges. Each pulse lerps from→to along its edge on a loop (`speed=0.15`, so ~6.7s per traversal) with a per-edge phase offset (`i / EDGES.length`) so edges don't pulse in sync. Scale modulated by `Math.sin(phase·π)` so the pulse fades in at the source node, peaks mid-edge, fades out at the destination — a "comet" feel. Color: coral for pipeline edges (both endpoints in `HIGHLIGHT`, i.e. the INPUT→PARSER→GRAPH→QA chain), emerald for everything else. `emissiveIntensity=2.4`, `toneMapped=false`, `frustumCulled=false`.
